Project 02 · Healthcare Analytics

The Economics of Early Detection

Chronic Disease Burden & Healthcare Sustainability in Saudi Arabia. This capstone combined Saudi Ministry of Health open data with published scientific literature to examine how chronic disease—particularly diabetes and its complications—can create long-term financial pressure, and where earlier detection and prevention could reduce downstream treatment costs.

3 · Downstream Cost Impact

Where Savings Could Materialize

Diabetes complications generate costs across multiple hospital departments. Addressing the underlying disease earlier could reduce downstream complications and associated treatment costs.

Nephrology

Core insight The value of prevention is not limited to one department. Reducing diabetes progression can lower the need for treatment across several downstream clinical services.
4 · Analytical Approach

How the Analysis Was Built

The project combined open datasets and research evidence rather than relying on a single source or a predictive machine-learning model.

Data Saudi Ministry of Health open data and historical budget information.
Research Published scientific literature was used for chronic disease economic-burden estimates.
Projection CAGR was calculated from historical budget trends and applied as a continuation-of-trend scenario through 2030.
